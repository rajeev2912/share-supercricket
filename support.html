Frequently Asked Questions


How do I start scoring a match in SuperCrik?

To score a match, navigate to your Dashboard and tap the Start Match button. You can choose to start a quick match or link it to an ongoing tournament. Select the batting/bowling teams, set the match configuration (overs, balls per over, wide rules), name your squads, and flip the coin to begin scoring ball-by-ball.

Can I add new players to squads mid-match?

Yes. If a squad member is missing or needs to be registered, you can tap the player registry from within the team configuration screen or select Add Player in the squad selection screen to dynamically register a profile and put them straight into the live squad.

How do I create and manage a tournament?

From the Dashboard, tap on the Tournaments tab and select Create Tournament. You can configure tournament details, round settings, groups, and add participating teams. You can also assign co-admins to help log match scorecards by searching for their username or phone number.

What happens if the app closes or I lose internet during a match?

Don't worry! SuperCrik stores match state on your local device. If the app closes or your phone runs out of battery, simply reopen the app, and you can resume right from the last ball scored. Once you regain internet access, the scorecard will automatically synchronize with our servers.

How do I share match updates or my player profile?

Every player profile, team page, and scorecard has a dedicated Share button (represented by a share icon). Tapping this allows you to automatically generate sharing links that friends can click to view your profile directly inside the app, or follow live matches ball-by-ball.
